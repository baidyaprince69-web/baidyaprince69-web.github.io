The person behind the strings

A familiar melody,
a different voice.

I'm Prince Baidya, the violinist behind Violin Prince. I create instrumental interpretations of songs that carry a feeling, a memory or a story worth returning to.

My musical world moves across Bengali songs, Rabindra Sangeet, Hindi melodies and English favourites. This website is the starting point for discovering the artist and the music; the dedicated collection is where the performances, moods and listening experiences come together.
